    Moon-Eater's Hollow

    Quest · Liurnia of the Lakes · Rewards the Eclipse-Bringer Greatsword

    Moon-Eater’s Hollow is a concept quest in Elden Ring that runs alongside Ranni the Witch’s own questline. It uses the same route, because both are Carian moon quests in Liurnia of the Lakes. Finishing it rewards the Eclipse-Bringer Greatsword.

    Concept content. The Moon-Eater’s Hollow, the Bakunawa Shade, the three Tide-Worn Scales, and the Bakunawa Fang are invented by Group 3 for GE008. Ranni’s Rise, Nokron, Renna’s Rise, the Ainsel River, the Raya Lucaria Grand Library, the Lake of Rot, Astel, and the Moonlight Altar are all real locations in the game.

    Prerequisites

    • Reached Liurnia of the Lakes.
    • Royal Knight Loretta beaten at Caria Manor, which opens the Three Sisters.
    • Have not yet reached the Altus Plateau. Reaching it starts the Radahn Festival early, and Ranni will not be in her tower.
    • Rune Level 70 or higher, with a main weapon at +6 or better.
    • Preserving Boluses, or the Flame, Cleanse Me incantation, for the Lake of Rot.

    Walkthrough

    1. Serve Ranni at Ranni’s Rise. Three Sisters, north-west Liurnia. Needs Royal Knight Loretta beaten. Talk to Ranni and agree to enter her service. Then speak to Iji, Blaidd, and Seluvis downstairs until their words repeat. Result: the barrier around the tower comes down.
    2. Beat Starscourge Radahn. Redmane Castle, Caelid. Result: the stars move again, one falls into Limgrave.
    3. Climb down into Nokron, Eternal City. Crash site south of Mistwood. Result: you arrive in Nokron.
    4. Take the three Tide-Worn Scales. Night’s Sacred Ground, under the huge skeleton statue. Push on to the Ancestral Woods Site of Grace first. Result: you hold three Tide-Worn Scales.
    5. Hand the Scales to Ranni. Result: she gives you the Carian Inverted Statue.
    6. Open the moon-phase seal and enter Renna’s Rise. Jump the rooftops and rocks from Ranni’s Rise to the next tower. Take the waygate at the top. Result: you arrive at the Ainsel River Main.
    7. Wake the doll. Pick up Miniature Ranni in the water, rest at the Site of Grace, and choose “Talk to miniature Ranni” three times. Result: she sends you after the shadow.
    8. Defeat the Bakunawa Shade. Follow the river on to the Nokstella Waterfall Basin. The Hollow opens off the river. Block the opening rush, then punish the pause. Result: you receive the Discarded Palace Key.
    9. Trade the key for the Fang. Raya Lucaria Grand Library, the small chest next to Rennala. Needs Rennala beaten. Result: you get the Bakunawa Fang.
    10. Cross the Lake of Rot. On to the Grand Cloister and ride the stone coffin. Result: you reach Astel.
    11. Beat Astel, Naturalborn of the Void. Result: the way out is open.
    12. Pass the sealed exit with the Fang. Result: you come out on the Moonlight Altar.
    13. Give the Fang to Ranni. Cathedral of Manus Celes. Drop down the hole near the Site of Grace and follow the tunnel to the old battle site. Result: the Eclipse-Bringer Greatsword is left behind.

    The two points where the quest breaks

    Warning, the festival If you reach the Altus Plateau or use the Grand Lift of Dectus before meeting Ranni, the Radahn Festival starts early and Ranni will not be at Ranni’s Rise. Blaidd, who is needed for the quest, will not leave the festival until Radahn is dead. The fix: beat Radahn, then come back. Ranni returns after he falls.
    Warning, the charm Handing the three Tide-Worn Scales to Ranni ends Preceptor Seluvis’s quest at once. Seluvis is the only source of the Magic Scorpion Charm, which is one of the three charms this guide recommends. Get the charm first.
