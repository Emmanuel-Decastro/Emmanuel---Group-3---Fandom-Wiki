    Moon-Devouring Tide

    Weapon Skill · 34 FP · 45 seconds · Magic and Frostbite

    Moon-Devouring Tide is the unique weapon skill of the Eclipse-Bringer Greatsword. It coats the blade in eclipsed moonlight for a short time. While it is on, the weapon gains magic attack power and Frostbite buildup, and fully charged heavy attacks release ranged crescent waves.

    In-game skill description (group-written concept text). “Raise the blade and let the eclipsed tide run down its fuller. Temporarily increases magic attack power and frostbite buildup; fully charged attacks release crescent waves of devoured moonlight.”

    Numbers

    PropertyValue
    FP cost34
    Length45 seconds

    Frostbite buildup38 rising to 55 while buffed
    Activation1.8 seconds, no hyper armour
    Wave reachAbout 18 metres, straight line

    Ends early ifThe weapon is stowed, swapped, or the player dies

    The cycle, in six stages

    1. Cast. Spend 34 FP and stand through a fixed 1.8 second activation. There is no hyper armour, so any hit stops it.
    2. Confirm. The groove down the blade glows cold white and frost flakes drift off it. No glow means the buff is not on.
    3. Buff. For 45 seconds you get +55 magic attack power, and Frostbite buildup rises from 38 to 55.
    4. Charge. Stand 8 to 18 metres away and hold the heavy attack button all the way. A partial charge fires nothing.
    5. Release. One crescent wave flies straight ahead, up to about 18 metres. It does Magic damage, 24 stance damage, and 83 Frostbite buildup. It costs stamina, not FP.
    6. Watch and repeat. At five seconds left the glow pulses. Make space and cast again. This is a loop, not a one-time action.

    The range band

    Keep the target between 8 and 18 metres away. Closer than 8 and a full charge will not finish. Further than 18 and the wave stops short. Stand on flat ground, because the wave travels straight and will hit a wall or a slope. Lead a moving target by hand, because the wave does not track.

    Notes

    • Only the activation costs FP. Firing waves costs stamina.
    • Because the buff is a weapon buff, it stacks with body and aura buffs.
    • Waves can be blocked by shields, and they can be parried with Golden Retaliation.
    • Next to the Dark Moon Greatsword’s Moonlight Greatsword, which costs 32 FP, lasts 60 seconds, and grants +65 magic attack power, this skill costs 2 more FP, lasts 15 seconds less, and grants 10 less magic attack power. Those changes pay for the lower Intelligence requirement and the better Guard Boost.
