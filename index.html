    Group 3 Elden Ring Wiki

    A student-made fan-concept wiki for GE008: The Alamat Project.

    Welcome to the Group 3 Elden Ring Wiki. This is a student-made, fan-concept wiki built for GE008: The Alamat Project. It documents one legendary armament that the group designed for Elden Ring, the Eclipse-Bringer Greatsword, together with its quest, its skill, and the Philippine eclipse myth it comes from.

    Read this first. The Eclipse-Bringer Greatsword, the Moon-Eater’s Hollow quest, the Bakunawa Shade, the Tide-Worn Scales, and the Bakunawa Fang are invented by Group 3. They are not official Elden Ring content. Every real location, item, and rule used on this wiki is taken from the game and is listed on the References page.

    About this wiki

    This wiki is the designed output for Technical Document #4. The brief asked for a beginner deployment guide written like a real Fandom page. The audience is new and inexperienced players, so the writing is plain, the steps are numbered, and game words are explained the first time they appear.

    Two sections were chosen, out of seven offered, because they are what a new player needs most:

    • How to get the weapon. The weapon sits behind one of the longest quests in the game, and one wrong step ends it for good.
    • How to use the weapon. The skill is a timed buff, and the waves only come from a fully charged heavy attack. Two conditions at once is where new players lose the weapon’s whole value.
